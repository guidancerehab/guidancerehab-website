Meet your PT

I built Guidance because I got tired of insurance deciding how people get treated. Rushed visits, double booked schedules, and a cap on how many sessions you get. That's not how real change happens. Most of getting better happens between visits, and that's where most PT goes quiet. Here I stay with you: a program that changes as you do, a quick check in every week, and unlimited messaging with me when something comes up. When you need a full visit, we book an hour, just you and me. The goal is change you can feel early, not months of going through the motions. I've treated a wide range of problems: vertigo and dizziness (BPPV), rehab after surgery, return to sport, chronic pain that won't quit, and the fresh tweak from last weekend. I've worked with UFC fighters and Cirque du Soleil performers, and plenty of people who just want to get back to everyday life without thinking about their body. I follow APTA clinical practice guidelines, track your progress with real numbers, and with your OK, send your doctor a short visit note.

Frequently asked questions

What happens on the free intro call?

15 minutes by phone. You tell me what's going on and what you want to get back to, and I tell you straight whether I can help and which option fits. No payment info, no pressure.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Nevada is a direct access state, so you can see your physical therapist without a referral.

Do you accept insurance?

No. Guidance is cash pay, which means more time with your PT, no visit limits, and care built around your goals instead of what insurance approves. I can provide a superbill for any visit, which you can submit to your insurance for possible out of network reimbursement.

Is membership worth it for me?

If you want a PT in your corner between visits, yes. Your first visit is included, then you get your program in the app, adjusted as you progress, unlimited messaging with me, and member pricing whenever you need a full visit. Most people book a full visit when something flares up or they hit a new goal.

Where do visits happen?

At my private, fully equipped gym in Las Vegas, by video, or at your home anywhere in the valley. No waiting room, no other patients, just you and me. You get the address when you book.

Can I cancel or pause?

Yes. Cancel anytime, or pause for a month from your Membership screen.

Who is this not for?

Emergencies, anyone who needs me to bill their insurance, and video visits for people outside Nevada. If something feels urgent or is getting worse fast, call your doctor or 911 first.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Physical therapy is usually an eligible expense for HSA and FSA accounts. Check with your plan to confirm what's covered.

What does manual therapy include?

Hands on work is part of every in person full visit when it fits. After a quick screen I use what fits you, such as joint manipulation, soft tissue work, IASTM, cupping or dry needling.

What is a superbill?

An itemized receipt with the details an insurance company asks for, like the service, date, and diagnosis and procedure codes. Since care here is cash pay and out of network, you can submit it to your insurance yourself to ask about reimbursement. Whether you get paid back depends on your plan. Members can download one anytime from the Membership screen.

How is this different from a personal trainer?

I'm a Doctor of Physical Therapy, so I can assess what's going on, treat it hands on, and measure your progress. A trainer can coach exercise, but can't diagnose or treat pain or injuries.

I have Hinge or Sword through work. Why pay for this?

Keep it if it helps. Those apps are mostly video exercises and remote coaching. Here you get a Doctor of Physical Therapy who actually looks at you, treats it hands on, and adjusts your plan as you progress. Lots of people use both.

What if I don't need a full visit?

After your included first visit, you only book one when you need it. Your program and messaging keep things moving, and you book a full visit at member pricing only when you need one.
