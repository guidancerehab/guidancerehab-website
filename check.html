Frequently asked questions

What happens on the free intro call?

15 minutes by phone or video. You tell me what's going on and what you want to get back to, and I tell you straight whether I can help and which option fits. No payment info, no pressure.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Nevada is a direct access state, so you can see your physical therapist without a referral.

Do you accept insurance?

No. Guidance is cash pay, which means more time with your PT, no visit limits, and care built around your goals instead of what insurance approves. I can provide a superbill for any visit, which you can submit to your insurance for possible out of network reimbursement.

Is membership worth it for me?

If you'd see me even once a month, yes. A single full visit is $175. Membership is $149 a month for the first 10 founding members ($179 after that), and that visit is already included, plus your program, messaging, and member pricing on anything extra. Add one tune up and it's $270 paying as you go vs $218 as a founding member.

Where do visits happen?

At my private, fully equipped gym in Las Vegas, by video, or at your home anywhere in the valley. No waiting room, no other patients, just you and me. You get the address when you book. Members: your included visit is at my gym or by video. Want me to come to you? Add a house call for $40 travel.

Can I cancel or pause?

Yes. Cancel anytime, or pause for a month from your Membership screen.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Physical therapy is usually an eligible expense for HSA and FSA accounts. Check with your plan to confirm what's covered.

What does manual therapy include?

Manual therapy is part of a hands on 30 minute tune up, alone or combined with dry needling. It starts with a quick screen, then I use the techniques that fit you, such as joint manipulation, soft tissue mobilization, IASTM, and cupping. Dry needling is booked separately.

What is a superbill?

An itemized receipt with the details an insurance company asks for, like the service, date, and diagnosis and procedure codes. Since care here is cash pay and out of network, you can submit it to your insurance yourself to ask about reimbursement. Whether you get paid back depends on your plan. Members can download one anytime from the Membership screen.

How is this different from a personal trainer?

I'm a Doctor of Physical Therapy, so I can assess what's going on, treat it hands on, and measure your progress. A trainer can coach exercise, but can't diagnose or treat pain or injuries.

I have Hinge or Sword through work. Why pay for this?

Keep it if it helps. Those apps are mostly video exercises and remote coaching. Here you get a Doctor of Physical Therapy who actually looks at you, treats it hands on, and adjusts your plan every week. Lots of people use both.

What if I don't use my monthly visit?

An unused visit carries over to the next month, then expires.
