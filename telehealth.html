Telehealth Consent

Some of your care happens by video and through the app. Please read this before your first remote visit.

Video visits through Doxy.me, program updates, messages, photos and form check videos reviewed by your physical therapist.

Telehealth saves travel and lets your PT adjust your plan more often.

Your PT cannot touch or fully examine you by video, so some problems may need an in person visit. Your PT may recommend an in person visit or a referral to another provider at any time.

Video care is provided to people located in Nevada at the time of the visit, where your PT is licensed. Tell your PT if you will be outside Nevada.

Video visits use Doxy.me, a telehealth platform built for healthcare. Use a private space and a secure connection. If a call drops, your PT will try to reconnect or call you.

Have a clear space to move, and someone nearby if you have fall risk. Stop if anything feels unsafe. In an emergency, call 911.

You may decline or stop telehealth at any time and choose in person care instead.

By checking the consent box at checkout or booking a video visit, you confirm you have read this, had the chance to ask questions, and agree to receive care by telehealth.
