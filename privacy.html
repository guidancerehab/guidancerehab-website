Privacy Policy

Guidance Rehab and Performance takes your privacy seriously. This explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Account details: name, email and optional phone.

Care details you choose to share: goals, equipment, weekly check ins (pain, soreness, effort, notes), messages, exercise completions, visit bookings and progress measures your PT records.

Payment details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Free body check: where it hurts, how it started, your age range, any health history you tick, and your goal. We use it to build your results and, if you book, to prepare for your call. If you ask us to email your results, we send them plus up to 2 follow up emails with tips, each with an unsubscribe link. A copy without your name or contact details is kept for 90 days so we can improve the check, then deleted. Ask us to delete it anytime at guidancerehabandperformance@gmail.com.

Website visits: we record page views and button taps on this site to learn what is useful. No ads and no third party trackers.

To provide and adjust your care, schedule visits, send sign in codes and visit reminders, process payments, and run the business. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers that run the app on our behalf: Supabase (secure database and sign in), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Wibbi (exercise programs and demo videos, which receives your name and email so your PT can build your program), Doxy.me (video visits) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They only use your information to provide their service to us.

We share information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Your PT keeps clinical records as required for licensed physical therapists in Nevada. Please do not send detailed medical history through the intro call form; we cover it during your visit. Access to your information in the app is limited to you and your PT.

Information is encrypted in transit, and each member can only see their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us right away if you think your account was accessed without permission.

You can view your information in the app, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account at guidancerehabandperformance@gmail.com. Some records must be kept for a period required by law.

We collect consumer health data only with your consent, such as when you agree before starting the body check, and only to provide the service you asked for. We never sell it.

You can ask to see what health data we have about you, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing guidancerehabandperformance@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; it does not undo care already provided.

Guidance is for adults 18 and over. Minors may be seen only with a parent or guardian who creates and manages the account.

Questions about privacy: guidancerehabandperformance@gmail.com.
