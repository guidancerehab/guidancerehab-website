Common questions

What makes it concierge physical therapy?

You get the same PT every time, for a full hour, with nobody else in the room. Visits happen where it suits you: my private gym, your home or by video. Between visits you can message me, and your program changes as you do.

How is this different from a regular PT clinic?

Most clinics book two or three patients an hour and hand parts of the visit to aides. Here the whole hour is yours, and the plan is built around your goals instead of what insurance will approve.

Do I need a referral or insurance?

No. Nevada is a direct access state, so you can book without a referral. Guidance is cash pay. I can give you a superbill to submit to your insurance for possible out of network reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards usually work.
